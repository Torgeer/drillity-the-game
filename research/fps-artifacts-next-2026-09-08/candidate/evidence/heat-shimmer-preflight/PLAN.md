## Corrected CDP chronology protocol

The old sign-only validation remains available for old capture behavior. New shimmer capture explicitly selects `cdp-signed-cumulative-v2`, based on primary V8/DevTools source pinned in the independent critic report. V8 serializes differences between retained samples; DevTools cumulatively reconstructs timestamps and sorts whole timestamp/sample pairs. Signed differences are therefore not inherently uninterpretable.

V2 retains every raw sample and delta unchanged. It checks signed safe integers, every intermediate cumulative timestamp against original profile bounds, node/sample references, nonempty chronological coverage and trace-mark integrity. It records every negative delta, every moved index and a stable sample-index permutation, including ties. It does not clamp/drop negative samples or sort deltas independently. The old reports retain their original overall rejection and are not retroactively accepted as particle experiments.

New captures also record `Browser.getVersion` protocol, product, revision and JavaScript-engine version. CPU-profile and Perfetto trace clocks are validated independently: no absolute clock equality or CPU-per-phase attribution is assumed without a measured clock-domain map. Per-frame synchronous CPU wrapper measurements remain separate from CDP samples.
