## 0. How to use this file

Every photograph listed in §2–§4 was **OPENED and looked at** — the observations
are what is actually in the frame, not what the filename suggests. That is the
honesty convention and it is absolute: an entry marked **OPENED** means the image
was put in front of my own eyes; anything not opened is marked **NOT OPENED**
with the reason, and the only such items are the 74 pattern-rejects in §5, which
are listed by pattern precisely so nobody re-opens them. Nothing here is inferred
from a filename.

Read §1 to find the one best image for your machine, §2 for the per-photo notes,
§3–§4 for the ground and the surface finishes that make a render look
photographed rather than modelled, §5 so you do not waste an hour on rejects, and
**§6 — the most important section, because it names the machines that have no
photograph at all and are therefore being modelled blind.**

Viewpoint is stated for every entry, because **an elevation is worth several
three-quarters**. `ELEVATION` = proportions can be taken off it directly.
`3/4` = silhouette and massing only. `DETAIL` = one component close up.
`CAD` = a shaded solid-model view (clean geometry, no branding, no wear).
`SCREEN` = a photograph of a monitor, or a screenshot (usable, but soft).

**The headline finding: the library is a tooling library, not a machine
library.** It is extraordinarily strong on bits, crowns, ring bits, casing and
threads, and it contains one genuinely excellent working-site sequence. Eleven of
the eighteen machine ids have nothing usable at all.

### `tools-overburden` — casing systems, ring bits, pilot bits, lost bits

By far the strongest family in the library. Read the CAD set first for geometry,
then the workshop photographs for finish, then the site photographs for wear.

#### The CAD set — clean geometry, no branding, no wear

Six shaded solid-model screenshots, all OPENED. `15.14.23`/`15.16.x` and
`08-18 12.10.x` are the **same six views sent twice** — open one set, not both.

**`C:\Users\henri\Downloads\WhatsApp Image 2026-08-06 at 15.16.08 (1).jpeg`**
(dup: `2026-08-18 at 12.10.21` is the 3/4, this is the elevation) — **CAD SIDE
ELEVATION of a casing crown / ring bit.** *The single most directly usable geometry

size.

## 4. Materials and wear — with the photograph each came from

- **Fresh weld against oxidised parent metal is the strongest contrast on any drilling
  tool.** In `WhatsApp Image 2026-06-01 at 11.46.36.jpeg` the weld beads at the base of
  every bar stub are **bright silver-white and entirely un-rusted**, standing proud in a
  rippled bead, while the casting around them is dark blue-grey mill scale under orange
